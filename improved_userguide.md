# ☀️ UK Weather App — User Guide

Welcome! This little app is your go-to for checking the weather across the UK — whether you're planning a BBQ, packing a suitcase, or simply wondering if you need an umbrella. Here's everything you need to know.

---

## 🔍 UK Weather Finder *(top card)*

Want to know what it's like right now in a specific place? This one's for you.

1. Type any UK location into the search box
2. Click **Search**

You'll get back the current **weather description**, **temperature**, **feels-like temperature**, **humidity**, and a handy **weather symbol** so you can see at a glance whether to grab a brolly.

> **💡 Tips:**
> - Don't worry about capitalisation — *london*, *London*, and *LONDON* all work fine.
> - Do double-check your spelling though — a typo will return no result.

---

## 🌡️ Hottest UK City Right Now *(bottom-left card)*

Curious where the sun's showing up today? One click is all it takes.

Hit the button and the app will instantly find the **hottest city in the UK at this moment**, returning the **city name**, **temperature**, **weather condition**, and a **weather symbol**.

Great for settling debates about which part of the country has the best weather (spoiler: it's rarely Scotland).

---

## 🏖️ Best UK Holiday Spot *(bottom-right card)*

Planning a trip and want the best weather? Let the app do the hard work.

1. Pick a **future date** using the date picker
2. Click **Find**

The app will return the UK city with the **highest temperature** *and* the **lowest chance of rain** on that date — plus a weather symbol to seal the deal.

> **📅 Note:** Forecasts are available up to **15 days ahead**. Dates beyond that won't return a result, so plan accordingly!

---

## ⏳ A Note on Loading

After clicking any button, keep an eye out for a **spinning sun** — that means your results are on their way. It can take a moment or two, so sit tight!

Oh, and all buttons turn **red when hovered** — just so you know you're in the right spot before you click. 🔴
